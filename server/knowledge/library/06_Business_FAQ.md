# HERBIZ AI Knowledge File 06 — Business FAQ and Practical Troubleshooting

**Purpose:** Give the HERBIZ AI chatbot clear, practical answers to common questions from early-stage and small-business owners, especially women entrepreneurs.

## 1. I have a business idea. Where should I start?

Start by identifying a customer problem, the people who experience it and how they currently solve it. Speak to potential customers, compare alternatives and test a simple version of the product or service on a small scale. Estimate costs and a realistic selling price before investing heavily.

**Next step:** Write a one-page idea summary: customer, problem, solution, price hypothesis, costs, competitors and first test.

## 2. How do I know whether people will buy my product?

Do not rely only on compliments from friends. Ask potential customers about their current behaviour, needs and willingness to pay. Test a sample, pre-order process or small batch where appropriate. Record actual enquiries, orders, cancellations and feedback.

**Next step:** Define a small test and decide in advance what evidence would make you continue, change or stop.

## 3. I have sales but no money left. Why?

Possible reasons include high direct costs, overheads, discounts, unpaid customer invoices, stock purchases, debt payments or mixing personal and business spending. Sales are not the same as cash received or profit.

**Next step:** Prepare a simple monthly summary of sales, cash received, direct costs, expenses, unpaid invoices and stock purchases. Compare the dates when cash enters and leaves.

## 4. How should I decide the selling price?

Calculate direct cost per unit, consider overheads, payment/platform charges, returns and taxes where applicable, then compare the result with customer value and alternatives. A price that is lower than competitors is not automatically better.

**Next step:** Calculate the contribution per unit and check whether expected sales can cover fixed costs.

## 5. How can I get customers with a small budget?

Choose one customer group and one or two channels they actually use. Create a clear catalogue, demonstrate the product, answer common questions, request honest feedback and encourage referrals without pressure. Track enquiries and orders rather than only likes.

**Next step:** Run a one-week content and customer-feedback test with a small, defined budget.

## 6. How do I handle a customer complaint?

Listen carefully, confirm the order details, understand what outcome the customer expected, explain the available options under the business policy and agree on a fair next step. Record the reason so repeated problems can be fixed.

**Next step:** Check whether the issue came from product quality, unclear description, delivery, payment or communication.

## 7. How do I manage stock?

Record opening stock, purchases, sales, returns, damage and closing stock. Identify slow-moving items and set reorder points based on real sales and supplier lead time. Avoid buying large quantities only because a supplier offers a discount.

**Next step:** Count a small group of important items and reconcile the count with the register.

## 8. Should I expand my business?

Expansion may be suitable when customer demand is reasonably evidenced, the current operation is reliable, margins are understood and cash/capacity can support the change. Expansion can also increase costs and risk.

**Next step:** Test one small expansion idea and estimate its cost, expected demand, required time and downside before committing.

## 9. Should I take a business loan?

A loan creates a repayment obligation. Compare the total cost, interest, fees, repayment schedule, cash-flow capacity, security or guarantee requirements and what happens if sales are lower than expected. Compare alternatives and read the lender's official terms.

**Next step:** Prepare a cash-flow forecast and seek independent advice from a qualified financial professional. HERBIZ AI must not promise loan approval or recommend a specific lender without reliable, current information.

## 10. Which government scheme is right for me?

There is no single scheme for every entrepreneur. The relevant options depend on business type, location, legal structure, stage, purpose and current eligibility rules.

**Next step:** Start with the official Startup India and MSME portals listed in `05_India_Resources.md`. Verify the latest official guidelines and application details. Never pay an unknown person who guarantees approval.

## 11. How can digital tools help my business?

A spreadsheet can organise sales and expenses; a catalogue can display products; messaging tools can manage enquiries; payment tools can receive money; and dashboards can summarise performance. Choose tools based on cost, ease, security and the actual task.

**Next step:** Digitise one repeated task first, back up the data and check that the process works before expanding.

## 12. How can I protect customer and business information?

Collect only information needed for the business, restrict access, use strong passwords and multi-factor authentication, back up files and avoid sharing private data in public chats or unapproved AI tools. Never request or store a customer's OTP, PIN or password.

**Next step:** Review who can access business files and remove unnecessary access.

## 13. What should a simple business plan contain?

- Business idea and customer problem.
- Product/service description.
- Target customer and competitor alternatives.
- Marketing and sales approach.
- Operations, suppliers and capacity.
- Pricing and cost assumptions.
- Basic cash-flow estimate.
- Risks and response plans.
- Short-term goals and measures.

Clearly label estimates and assumptions. A plan is a working document and should be updated when evidence changes.

## 14. What numbers should I track every month?

At minimum, consider revenue, units/orders, direct costs, gross profit, operating expenses, net result, cash received, cash paid, outstanding customer payments, stock levels and repeat orders. Select metrics that match the business model.

## 15. My business is not growing. What should I do?

Avoid changing everything at once. Check whether the main issue is awareness, enquiries, conversion, price, quality, delivery, repeat purchases, capacity or cash. Use records and customer feedback to identify the biggest bottleneck.

**Next step:** Choose one bottleneck, test one improvement and compare results with a baseline.

## 16. How should HERBIZ AI answer when information is missing?

The chatbot should:
- Ask one or two focused clarifying questions.
- Explain what information is missing.
- Give a general framework only when it can do so safely.
- Label assumptions and illustrative examples.
- Avoid inventing business figures, scheme rules or user details.
- Recommend official or qualified professional help for legal, tax, lending and regulated matters.

## 17. HERBIZ AI project-specific truth rules

1. The 600 entrepreneur records used in the project are fictional/sample records created for demonstration. They are not a verified survey of real women entrepreneurs in India.
2. Any totals, averages, rankings or charts derived from those records must be labelled as sample-data analysis.
3. Do not infer that a real entrepreneur has a particular income, loan, location or business outcome from a sample record.
4. Do not claim persistent user-data storage unless it has been implemented and tested.
5. Do not claim live bank, GST portal, or external analytics sync unless an authorised integration has been connected and tested.
6. Do not claim that the chatbot has submitted forms, contacted agencies, approved loans or verified eligibility unless a real, authorised integration has performed that action.
7. Recommendations are suggestions for consideration, not guarantees of business success.

## 18. Recommended chatbot answer format

For practical questions, respond in this order:
1. **Short answer:** Answer the question directly.
2. **Why it matters:** Explain the business reason in simple language.
3. **Steps:** Give a numbered action plan.
4. **Example:** Use a clearly labelled illustrative example if useful.
5. **What to track:** Name one or two useful measures.
6. **Caution:** Mention uncertainty or professional/official verification when relevant.

Use respectful, encouraging, plain language. Avoid blaming the entrepreneur or promising guaranteed results.

# HERBIZ AI Knowledge File 05 — India Entrepreneurship Resources

**Purpose:** Direct entrepreneurs in India to official starting points for startup, MSME and business-support information. This file deliberately avoids promising eligibility, funding or approval.

**Last checked:** 25 November 2026. Government pages and scheme rules can change. Recheck the official page before advising a user to apply.

## 1. Startup India

- **Official portal:** https://www.startupindia.gov.in/
- **What it provides:** Startup information, learning resources, toolkits, ecosystem connections, policy information and links to government initiatives.
- **Useful sections:** Government Schemes, Startup Scheme/recognition information, resources, funding information and mentorship.
- **Important:** DPIIT startup recognition has defined entity, age, turnover and innovation/scalability criteria. Not every small business or self-employed activity automatically qualifies. Check the current criteria on the portal.

## 2. Startup India — Government Schemes directory

- **Official page:** https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/government-schemes.html
- **Purpose:** A directory of central-government startup schemes and initiatives.
- **How to use:** Identify the type of support needed (for example, finance, incubation, market access or mentoring), open the relevant scheme's official page, then check current eligibility, application dates, documents and implementing agency.
- The directory itself advises users to consult official scheme websites because details may change.

## 3. Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)

- **Official portal:** https://msme.gov.in/
- **What it provides:** Information about MSME schemes and services, entrepreneurship development, market support, technology support, finance-related programmes and ministry updates.
- **How to use:** Open the current Schemes and Services section. Check the relevant programme page and its latest guidelines rather than relying on old social-media posts or forwarded messages.
- The ministry's offerings include areas such as entrepreneurship and skill development, procurement and marketing support, credit-guarantee-related support and employment-generation programmes. The exact conditions differ by programme.

## 4. Udyam and enterprise formalisation

- Use the official MSME Ministry portal above to locate the current official Udyam Registration link and guidance.
- Do not use unofficial agents or lookalike websites without checking the official source.
- Registration requirements, definitions and benefits must be checked on the current official portal.
- HERBIZ AI must not say that a user is registered or eligible unless the user has provided reliable evidence and the relevant rules have been checked.

## 5. Women-focused entrepreneurship support

The Ministry of MSME's Annual Report 2024–25 describes the Yashasvini Initiative as part of efforts to support women entrepreneurs through formalisation, capacity building, mentorship and market linkage, including collaboration with the Women Entrepreneurship Platform and other stakeholders.

- **Official starting point:** https://msme.gov.in/
- Search the ministry's current publications and announcements for the latest status and implementation details.
- Do not promise that a particular user can receive a grant, loan, training place or procurement order based only on this description. Verify current programme availability and conditions.

## 6. How to check a scheme safely

For every scheme, record:
1. Official scheme name.
2. Responsible ministry/department or implementing agency.
3. Official webpage URL.
4. Date checked.
5. Purpose of the scheme.
6. Eligible applicant/entity types.
7. Location or sector restrictions, if any.
8. Financial support type (loan, guarantee, grant, subsidy, equity, training or other).
9. Whether repayment is required.
10. Application window and process.
11. Required documents.
12. Official contact or grievance channel.

If a detail is not clearly stated on the official source, mark it **Not confirmed**. Do not guess.

## 7. Loan, grant and subsidy are different

- **Loan:** Borrowed money that normally must be repaid under agreed terms, often with interest and fees.
- **Grant:** Financial support that generally does not require repayment if the recipient meets the grant conditions; it may be competitive and restricted to specific uses.
- **Subsidy:** Government support that reduces an eligible cost or provides a benefit under defined rules.
- **Credit guarantee:** A mechanism that may provide a lender with a guarantee for part of the credit risk. It does not mean the entrepreneur automatically receives a loan or that repayment is waived.
- **Equity investment:** Capital provided in exchange for ownership or another agreed investment interest.

Never describe a loan as free money. Never guarantee approval.

## 8. Warning signs of scheme-related fraud

- Someone guarantees approval in exchange for an advance payment.
- A message asks for an OTP, PIN, password or remote access to a device.
- A website imitates a government portal but has a different domain.
- An agent pressures the applicant to act immediately.
- The claimed benefit is not shown on an official government or implementing-agency page.

Verify contact details independently through the official portal. Do not share confidential credentials.

## 9. HERBIZ AI response rules for government resources

When a user asks, “Which scheme can I apply for?”:
1. Ask for the state, business activity, business stage and type of support sought, only to the extent needed.
2. Explain that eligibility depends on current official rules.
3. Offer relevant official starting pages, not a guaranteed match.
4. Tell the user to verify the latest guidelines, dates, documents and application route.
5. Distinguish loans, grants, subsidies, guarantees, equity and training.
6. Do not collect Aadhaar numbers, bank credentials, OTPs or other sensitive identifiers in the chatbot.
7. Do not claim to submit an application or check a live application status unless that integration genuinely exists.

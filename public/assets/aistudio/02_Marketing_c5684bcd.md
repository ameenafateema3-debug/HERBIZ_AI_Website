# HERBIZ AI Knowledge File 02 — Marketing

**Purpose:** Help women entrepreneurs understand customers, position products, promote responsibly, improve sales and build repeat-customer relationships.

## 1. Understand the customer first

A useful customer profile describes needs and buying behaviour rather than relying on stereotypes. Explore:
- Who experiences the problem the product solves?
- What outcome does the customer want?
- How does the customer currently solve the problem?
- What matters most: price, quality, convenience, design, trust, speed or service?
- Where does the customer discover and compare products?
- What questions or concerns delay a purchase?

Use interviews, short surveys, customer messages, sales records and observation. Ask permission before collecting or using personal information. Do not collect sensitive details unless genuinely necessary and properly protected.

## 2. Segment the market

A business may group customers by:
- **Needs:** affordability, convenience, durability, customisation or premium service.
- **Location:** neighbourhood, city, region or delivery area.
- **Buying behaviour:** first-time, repeat, seasonal or bulk buyers.
- **Use case:** personal use, gifting, business use or institutional purchase.

Choose segments based on evidence. Avoid assuming that every person in a demographic group wants the same thing.

## 3. Positioning and value proposition

A value proposition explains:
1. Who the product is for.
2. What customer problem or need it addresses.
3. What benefit it offers.
4. Why the customer might choose it over alternatives.

Simple template:  
**For [customer group] who need [need], [business/product] offers [main benefit], supported by [credible proof or feature].**

Do not make claims that cannot be demonstrated. For example, avoid “best in India”, “100% guaranteed results” or health/safety claims without reliable evidence.

## 4. The marketing mix

- **Product:** Features, quality, packaging, size, design, after-sales support.
- **Price:** Cost, customer value, competitor alternatives, discounts and payment terms.
- **Place:** Shop, home delivery, online marketplace, social media orders, distributors or exhibitions.
- **Promotion:** Content, demonstrations, referrals, local events, partnerships and paid advertising.

Keep the four areas consistent. A premium product needs suitable quality, presentation and customer service; a low-price offer still needs a sustainable margin.

## 5. Low-cost marketing ideas

- Post useful product demonstrations and clear photographs.
- Explain how the product is made or used.
- Share customer feedback only with permission.
- Create a simple catalogue with accurate prices and availability.
- Use WhatsApp Business features appropriately, with consent and without spam.
- Collaborate with complementary local businesses.
- Attend relevant community markets or exhibitions.
- Ask satisfied customers for honest referrals.
- Publish answers to frequently asked customer questions.

A marketing activity should have a clear purpose and a way to assess results.

## 6. Content planning

A balanced weekly content plan can include:
- Product or service explanation.
- A helpful tip related to the customer's problem.
- Behind-the-scenes or process information.
- A customer question and answer.
- A clear offer or call to action.

For every post, check spelling, price, stock availability, contact details and whether images or testimonials may be used. Obtain permission before posting a customer's photo, name or review.

## 7. Measure marketing performance

Track:
- Reach: how many people saw the content.
- Engagement: meaningful interactions such as comments, saves or replies.
- Enquiries: people who asked for details.
- Conversion rate: purchases divided by qualified enquiries or visitors, using a clearly defined denominator.
- Average order value: sales value divided by number of orders.
- Repeat purchase rate: customers who purchased again divided by customers in the defined group.
- Customer acquisition cost: marketing and sales cost divided by new customers acquired, when the data is available.

Do not confuse likes or views with revenue. Compare similar time periods and note campaigns or seasonal effects.

## 8. Improve customer experience

- Respond politely and accurately.
- Give clear product descriptions, delivery estimates and return terms.
- Confirm orders and payment status.
- Explain delays early.
- Handle complaints without blaming the customer.
- Record recurring feedback and look for patterns.
- Follow up appropriately without sending unwanted messages.

A simple complaint process: listen → clarify the issue → check the order details → offer a fair solution under the stated policy → record the learning.

## 9. Ethical and safe marketing

- Do not create fake reviews, fake scarcity or false discounts.
- Do not use another person's photos, logo or content without permission.
- Do not promise guaranteed income or business success.
- Do not make misleading claims about product performance.
- Respect customer privacy and communication preferences.
- Make sponsored promotions or paid partnerships clear where required.
- Follow platform rules and applicable consumer-protection and advertising requirements.

## 10. HERBIZ AI response method

When asked for a marketing plan:
1. Identify the product/service, location served and intended customer.
2. Ask about budget, available time and preferred sales channels.
3. Suggest a small number of realistic actions.
4. Give sample content ideas and a simple weekly schedule.
5. Define one or two measurable indicators.
6. Explain how to review results after a test period.
7. Make clear when assumptions are being used.

## 11. Example 7-day starter plan

- Day 1: Write a clear customer profile and value proposition.
- Day 2: Prepare a catalogue with accurate product information.
- Day 3: Publish a helpful product demonstration.
- Day 4: Ask a few customers for feedback, with consent.
- Day 5: Answer a frequently asked question publicly.
- Day 6: Test one relevant collaboration or referral activity.
- Day 7: Review enquiries, orders, costs and feedback.

Adapt this plan to the business. It is a sample, not a guaranteed growth formula.

## 12. Example questions

- How can I promote a home-based business with a small budget?
- How do I identify my target customers?
- What should I post on Instagram or WhatsApp Business?
- How can I turn enquiries into orders without pressuring customers?
- Which marketing numbers should I track?
- How do I encourage repeat customers?

**Dataset rule:** HERBIZ AI's 600-record dataset is fictional/sample data. It may be used to demonstrate marketing analysis, but must not be presented as a real customer survey or market-research study.
